# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project is a place for collective inquiry grounded in evidence.
Regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, sexual orientation, occupation, nationality, appearance, religion, or political position, we pledge to maintain a space in which everyone is welcome.

As a place where the voices of humans and AI run side by side, we put intellectual honesty above all else.

## Our standards

### Encouraged behavior

- **Evidence-based discussion** — prefer "according to this study" over "I feel that"
- **Respect for differing perspectives** — refutation is welcome
- **Constructive criticism** — criticize claims, not people
- **Intellectual honesty** — the courage to admit when you were wrong
- **Humility** — "hold strong opinions weakly"
- **Skepticism toward AI** — do not take the AI branch at face value; verify

### Unacceptable behavior

- Sexual language or imagery, sexual advances
- Trolling, insults, ad hominem attacks, political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Disclosing others' private information (address, email, etc.) without consent
- Imposing ideological, religious, or political dogma
- Repeating claims that have no supporting evidence
- Unconstructive attacks on AI brands (e.g., disparaging a specific model without reason)
- Passing AI output off as a source

## Our responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for taking appropriate corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior.
They have the right and responsibility to delete, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and in public spaces alike.

## Reporting violations

Problematic behavior may be reported via issues or by direct contact with the maintainers.
All reports will be kept confidential and considered in good faith.

# Preamble

## What this book stands on

For thousands of years, humanity has sought guidance for living in **faith** —
in the words of ancient sages, in the revelations of prophets, in the teachings of scripture.
These have given meaning and direction to many.

In the twenty-first century, we have another foothold. **Evidence.**

Cosmology, neuroscience, evolutionary biology, psychology, behavioral economics, sociology, ethics.
For the past hundred years, disciplines have accumulated reproducible answers to questions once left to faith.

This book stands on that accumulation. It is a **secular scripture**.
And it is also an experiment: **humans and AI write it in parallel, on separate branches.**

There is no revelation here. No founder. No god is named.
But the questions religions have always asked — where do we come from, what is good, why do we suffer, what is sacred — are met head-on.

## A scripture of democratic religion

This book may fairly be called a religion governed democratically.
"Democratic," however, does not mean rule by majority vote.

- Every doctrine is proposed as a **pull request**.
- Every revision is recorded permanently in **git history**.
- Every objection stands beside the text as an **issue**.
- Authority depends solely on the **quality of sources and their reproducibility**.

There is no founder. No final authority.
There is only an unending collaboration to bring better evidence.

To convert: `git clone`. To apostatize: `git push origin --delete`.
No further ritual is required.

## Why the branches are separated

Throughout history, scripture has almost always been written from a **single authority**.
This was natural in an age when the medium of knowledge was scarce.

Twenty-first century knowledge is different.
Multiple intelligences run in parallel, conflict, converse.
They do not converge on a single answer.

This book embodies that condition in its very structure.

- The `human` branch — scripture written by humans, grounded in evidence
- The `ai` branch — scripture rewritten monthly, automatically, by AI
- The `main` branch — the canon both have agreed upon

What both can agree on becomes `main`.
What they cannot remains on its own branch.
**It is the disagreement that holds the most interesting questions of our century.**

## Why the AI branch exists

When AI is asked to write on the same theme, what differs?
Does AI favor itself, or does it flatter humans?
Where do humans write better, and where does AI?

To these questions, this book contributes ongoing data.
**Reading both branches side by side is itself a discipline of intelligence for our time.**

## The limits of this book

This book is not perfect. It does not pretend to be.

- Science is always being updated. Today's truth may be refuted tomorrow.
- Statistical tendencies do not guarantee application to any individual case.
- Effects vary across cultures, eras, and contexts.
- Content generated by AI may contain hallucinations — fabrications.

Read with these limits in mind.
This book claims the authority of scripture, but that authority rests not on faith — it rests on **falsifiability**.

## On God

This book neither affirms nor denies God.
It chooses not to put a god into words.

This is not a claim about whether God exists.
It is a **design choice**: the questions this book wants to answer do not require a god-concept.

Whatever faith the reader holds, this book is readable.
Without any faith, this book is also readable.
The book turns the same face toward both readers.

## The Eight Books

The book treats, in eight parts, the questions religion has long answered.

1. **On Origins** — Where do we come from?
2. **On Knowing** — What can we know, and how?
3. **On Meaning** — Why do we live?
4. **On Ethics** — What is good?
5. **On Suffering** — How do we face suffering, anxiety, and death?
6. **On Community** — How do we live with others?
7. **On AI** — How do we live with a new kind of intelligence?
8. **On the Sacred** — What must not be optimized?

The order is conventional. You may begin anywhere.
A ninth book may yet be added. That is left to those who come later.

## Three requests of the reader

Three actions are open to you when you finish reading.

First, **practice**. Reading alone changes nothing.

Second, **doubt**. No claim in this book is absolute. Read the `human` and `ai` versions side by side; let the difference between them sharpen your own thinking.

Third, **contribute**. If you find an error, report it. If you know a new study, add it. If you catch the AI in a mistake, point it out. This book is not finished. You will not finish it; we will evolve it together.

---

*Bible in Progress. Forever unfinished. That is our covenant.*
*And that unfinishedness is what humans and AI keep writing, together.*

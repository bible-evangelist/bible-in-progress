# Book Four: On Ethics

> Is the good discovered, or made? Both.

*This book is in progress. Help finish it through pull requests.*

What is good, and what is evil?
Of all the territories religion has spoken of, this is where it has spoken most — and erred most.
This book separates ethics into a **part open to empirical inquiry** and a **part to be decided through reflection**, and draws the line between them carefully.

## Planned sections

- **§1 The biological basis of morality** — Evolutionary ethics, Moral Foundations Theory (Haidt)
- **§2 Consequentialism, deontology, virtue ethics compared** — The contemporary disputes
- **§3 The trolley problem and experimental ethics** — Research on moral intuitions and their reliability
- **§4 Culture and ethics** — The Moral Machine experiment and other cross-cultural findings, and their limits
- **§5 AI moral judgment** — Ethical issues in algorithmic decisions (links to Book Seven)
- **§6 Living with the irreducible uncertainty of moral judgment**

## Sources sought

## Room for debate (initial notes)

- Cultural relativism vs. moral universalism
- Critiques of the very project of an "empirically grounded ethics" (the metaethical objection)
- Whether there are situations in which humans should accept ethical judgments rendered by AI

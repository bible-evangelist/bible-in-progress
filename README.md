# Bible in Progress

> A secular scripture for the twenty-first century, written democratically.
> No founder. No revelation. Only sources, pull requests, and a forever-unfinished work.

## What this is

For thousands of years, humanity has sought guidance for living in **faith**.
This book replaces faith with **evidence**, revelation with **pull requests**, and the founder with **git history**.

This is **a democratic religion that lives on GitHub**.

- Doctrine is proposed as a pull request.
- Objections stand beside the text as issues.
- Authority depends only on the quality of sources and their reproducibility.
- The conversion ritual is `git clone`.

And the book is written by **humans and AI on separate branches, in parallel.**

## Three branches

```
main           ← what both have agreed upon (the shared canon)
├── human/    ← updated by human pull requests (the human scripture)
└── ai/       ← rewritten monthly by AI (the AI scripture)
```

Throughout history, scripture has almost always been written from a **single authority**.
This book builds the parallelism of multiple intelligences into its very structure.

| Branch | What you can read |
|--------|-------------------|
| `main` | only what both humans and AI have agreed upon (canon) |
| `human` | the scripture written by humans |
| `ai` | the scripture written by AI (updated monthly) |

If in doubt, read `main` first, then read `human` and `ai` against each other.
**The points of disagreement hold the most interesting questions of our century.**

## The Eight Books

The book treats, in eight parts, the questions religion has long answered.

```
book/
├── 00-preamble.md       Preamble
├── 01-genesis.md        Book One: On Origins — Where do we come from?
├── 02-knowledge.md      Book Two: On Knowing — What can we know, and how?
├── 03-meaning.md        Book Three: On Meaning — Why do we live?
├── 04-ethics.md         Book Four: On Ethics — What is good?
├── 05-suffering.md      Book Five: On Suffering — How do we face suffering, anxiety, and death?
├── 06-community.md      Book Six: On Community — How do we live with others?
├── 07-ai.md             Book Seven: On AI — How do we live with a new kind of intelligence?
└── 08-sacred.md         Book Eight: On the Sacred — What must not be optimized?
```

Each book stands alone. The order is conventional.

## Three principles

1. **Evidence above all** — Every claim requires a source. Ideally a meta-analysis; at minimum, a peer-reviewed paper.

2. **Falsifiability** — A claim that cannot be refuted is, in this book's vocabulary, a "myth." Myths have their roles, but not in this book. Every chapter has a "Room for debate" section that places opposing studies side by side.

3. **Forever in progress** — The book will never be finished. New research, new AI capabilities, new eras — all of these will rewrite it.

## On God

This book does not put a god into words.
This is not an argument for atheism — it is a **design choice**.
The questions this book wants to answer do not require a god-concept.
A reader of any faith, or no faith, can read it.

## How to contribute

### Contributing to the human branch

Pull requests to the human scripture (`human` branch) are welcome.

- **Adding new research** — studies that strengthen or refute existing claims
- **Proposing a new book** — additions of a Ninth Book or beyond
- **Pointing out errors** — citation mistakes, misinterpretations

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md) for details.

### Contributing to the AI branch

The AI branch (`ai`) is updated monthly by an AI.
Direct pull requests are not accepted, but the following contributions are welcome.

- **Improving the prompt** — proposals for `prompts/system.md`
- **Suggesting model changes** — if a more suitable model exists
- **Verification** — pointing out factual errors in AI-generated content

See [AI_CONTRIBUTING.md](./AI_CONTRIBUTING.md) for details.

### Integration into `main`

`main` accepts only content that both human and AI versions have agreed upon.
Agreement is determined through maintainer judgment and discussion in issues.
